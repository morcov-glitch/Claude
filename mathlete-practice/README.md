# Mathlete Training

A practice web app for Year 8 students at the International School of Brno, preparing for the Term 1 Mathlete Competition (Tuesday 24 November 2026).

Open `index.html` in any browser. It needs no build step or server.

- **Round 1 · Individual Challenge**: 10 random problems from a bank of 68 extended Year 8 questions, mixed or filtered by topic, with hints, worked solutions and a timer.
- **Round 2 · Team Logic & Puzzles**: 18 brain-teasers with difficulty ratings, hints and solved-progress tracking.
- **Round 3 · Relay Arithmetic Race**: generated 5-leg relay chains (each answer feeds the next leg) and a 60-second mental arithmetic sprint.

Best scores and solved puzzles are saved in the browser's localStorage. `app.html` is the same page without the document wrapper, used for the published Artifact version.
