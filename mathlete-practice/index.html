On the day

Bring enthusiasm, focus and team spirit. Arrive on time so the morning can start promptly.

  1. Read the whole question twice. Mathlete questions hide the key fact in one word: "at least", "guarantee", "consecutive".
  2. Write down working. Reasoning counts in the Individual round, and neat working helps you catch slips.
  3. In the team round, split then share. Two people try the puzzle; one checks the answer makes sense.
  4. In the relay, check before you pass. A wrong number ruins every leg after yours. A two-second check is cheaper.
  5. Estimate first. If 18 × 23 should be "about 400", an answer of 4140 is clearly wrong.
